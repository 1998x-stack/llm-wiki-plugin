In financial services, AI agents are moving beyond pilot programs to deliver concrete business value.在金融服务领域，人工智能智能体正从试点项目走向落地，为企业带来实实在在的业务价值。

In banking, wealth management, and insurance, autonomous AI agents are transforming how customers understand spending patterns and find savings opportunities. Among other use cases, these tools spot potential overdraft fees, suggest better savings strategies, and guide financial decisions. 在银行、财富管理和保险领域，自主式人工智能智能体正在改变客户理解消费模式和寻找储蓄机会的方式。在其他应用场景中，这些工具可以识别潜在的透支费用，推荐更优的储蓄策略，并指导财务决策。

For instance, McKinsey research shows that financial institutions adopting AI agent workflows in fraud detection could generate [two hundred to two thousand percent productivity gains](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime), while Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM) employees [save hundreds of cumulative hours per week](https://claude.com/customers/nbim) on analytical and operational tasks using Claude. 例如，麦肯锡的研究显示，在欺诈检测中采用AI智能体工作流的金融机构可实现 [200%至2000%的生产力提升](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime) ，而挪威银行投资管理公司（NBIM）的员工借助Claude， [每周在分析和运营工作上累计节省数百小时](https://claude.com/customers/nbim) 。

For most organizations, the real challenge isn't adopting AI agents. It's building systems that navigate complex regulations, manage real-time risk, and protect customer assets while improving business outcomes.对于大多数组织而言，真正的挑战并非采用AI智能体，而是构建能够在提升业务成果的同时，应对复杂法规、管理实时风险并保护客户资产的系统。

## Moving beyond analysis: how agents change the game超越分析：智能体如何改变游戏规则

Agents represent a fundamental shift in enterprise AI, replacing generative AI tools that depend upon constant human input and oversight with [autonomous systems](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-financial-services) that handle long-running, context-heavy tasks with minimal – if any – intervention. 智能体代表了企业人工智能的根本性转变，它取代了那些依赖持续人工输入和监督的生成式人工智能工具，转而采用 [自主系统](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-financial-services) ，这些系统只需极少的人工干预——即便完全无需人工干预，也能处理耗时长久、涉及大量上下文信息的任务。

This evolution is especially welcome in financial services, where data often lives in fragmented systems that don't talk to each other, making it harder to see the complete picture of a customer's financial health. Agentic systems can understand financial context, ingest information from multiple unrelated sources, process multiple kinds of data (transaction records, market data, regulatory documents), and apply all of these capabilities to taking meaningful actions within your customer’s existing financial workflows.这一发展在金融服务领域尤为受欢迎，因为金融领域的数据通常存储在彼此无法交互的碎片化系统中，这使得人们更难全面了解客户的财务状况。智能体系统能够理解金融场景上下文，从多个不相关的来源获取信息，处理多种类型的数据（交易记录、市场数据、监管文件），并运用所有这些功能，在客户现有的金融业务流程中采取有意义的行动。

What does this look like in practice? Instead of an analyst manually pulling data from five different systems, reviewing it, and then updating a risk assessment, an agent can monitor transaction patterns across those systems, recognize concerning trends, draft updated risk recommendations based on current regulations, and route them to the right analyst for approval. The agent handles the coordination and analysis, while the analyst makes the final decision.这在实际中是什么样子呢？分析师不再需要手动从五个不同的系统中提取数据、进行审核，然后更新风险评估，智能体可以监控这些系统的交易模式，识别可疑趋势，根据现行法规起草更新后的风险建议，并将其发送给合适的分析师审批。智能体负责协调和分析工作，而分析师做出最终决策。

This shift from traditional AI to AI agents is particularly significant for financial services because it tackles the process completion problem. Financial workflows don't just need information, they need actions taken across multiple systems to actually complete transactions and maintain compliance. Agents can bridge those gaps.从传统人工智能到人工智能智能体的这一转变对金融服务行业意义尤为重大，因为它解决了流程完成这一难题。金融工作流不仅需要信息，还需要跨多个系统执行操作，才能真正完成交易并确保合规。智能体能够弥合这些差距。

## Real-world results AI agents in finance 金融领域AI智能体的实际应用成果

AI-powered financial agents are already delivering real-world results in areas such as customer service, fraud detection, and workforce empowerment.人工智能驱动的金融智能体已在客户服务、欺诈检测和员工赋能等领域取得了切实的实际成果。

### Transforming customer interactions 重塑客户互动

Customer service operations are a natural starting point because this area has already proven successful. Financial institutions implementing AI-powered customer service are seeing measurable improvements:客户服务运营是一个很自然的切入点，因为该领域已被证明成效显著。采用人工智能驱动客户服务的金融机构正取得可量化的改进：

- Multilingual virtual assistants now handle hundreds of millions of interactions annually, serving millions of users across different language groups.多语言虚拟助手如今每年处理数亿次交互，为不同语言群体的数百万用户提供服务。
- Customer service agents automate routine tasks like balance inquiries and card replacements, reducing wait times while delivering 24/7 responses and suggesting relevant products, turning support interactions into opportunities to actually help people manage their finances.客服坐席可自动化处理余额查询、补卡等常规任务，既缩短了等待时间，又能提供全天候响应，同时推荐相关产品，将客服互动转化为切实帮助用户管理个人财务的机会。

[Intuit TurboTax](https://claude.com/customers/intuit), for example, built an AI financial assistant powered by Claude that generates clear and accurate tax explanations for millions of customers. The agentic implementation was so successful that the AI-powered experiences achieved higher customer ratings compared to non-Claude experiences in the previous tax season. 例如， [Intuit TurboTax](https://claude.com/customers/intuit) 打造了一款由 Claude 驱动的人工智能财务助手，为数百万客户生成清晰准确的税务说明。这一智能体方案的实施效果极佳，在上个报税季，人工智能驱动的服务体验获得的客户评分高于非 Claude 驱动的服务体验。

### Combating fraud and financial crime 打击欺诈与金融犯罪

When you consider that financial institutions currently [catch only about 2%](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime) of global financial crime, you can see why this matters.在欺诈检测和网络犯罪预防领域，智能体擅长识别那些因数量过于庞大而可能被人类分析师忽略的模式。考虑到金融机构目前 [仅能抓获约 2%](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime) 的全球金融犯罪案件，你就能明白这为何至关重要了。

AI agents monitor millions of transactions in real-time, working around the clock without the fatigue or cognitive limitations that affect human teams. McKinsey found that [a single team member](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime) can effectively supervise more than 20 AI agents in financial crime-detection workflows.AI智能体实时监控数百万笔交易，全天候不间断工作，不会出现影响人类团队的疲劳或认知局限。麦肯锡发现， [单个团队成员](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime) 在金融犯罪检测流程中可以有效监督20个以上的AI智能体。

For [Brex](https://claude.com/customers/brex), a modern financial platform, Claude powers their AI anomaly detection, reviewing 100% percent of transactions and providing critical aircover for financial professionals by proactively grouping related expenses, flagging policy concerns, and providing explanations with recommended actions. 对于现代金融平台 [Brex](https://claude.com/customers/brex) 而言，Claude 为其人工智能异常检测功能提供支持，该功能会审核100%的交易，并通过主动对相关费用进行归类、标记政策问题以及附带建议操作的说明，为金融专业人士提供关键的支持保障。

### Amplifying team capabilities 强化团队能力

AI agents deliver tangible benefits right where your teams need them most. When implemented thoughtfully, these tools don't replace your workforce but rather amplify what they can accomplish and let them focus on the high-value work that requires their knowledge and expertise. Here's how financial services organizations like [Block](https://www.claude.com/customers/block) and [Campfire](https://claude.com/customers/campfire) are doing this:AI 智能体会在你的团队最需要的地方切实发挥作用。经过审慎部署后，这些工具不会取代你的员工，反而能增强他们的工作能力，让他们专注于需要自身知识和专业技能的高价值工作。以下是像 [Block](https://www.claude.com/customers/block) 和 [Campfire](https://claude.com/customers/campfire) 这类金融服务机构的实践方式：

- Accounting teams query financial data through natural language, perform flux analyses, and access audit support 会计团队通过自然语言查询财务数据、进行流量分析并获取审计支持

## Unique challenges in financial services金融服务领域的独特挑战

Banks and financial institutions face unique challenges that make AI agent implementation more complex than typical enterprise deployments. When every decision potentially impacts customer finances, regulatory compliance, and institutional risk, the stakes are at a different level altogether.银行和金融机构面临着独特的挑战，这使得AI智能体的实施比典型的企业部署更为复杂。当每一项决策都可能影响客户财务、合规要求和机构风险时，其风险等级完全不同。

The combination of complex financial contexts, regulatory requirements, and direct impact on customer outcomes creates an implementation environment where thoroughness trumps speed. These are some of the challenges you can expect to encounter.复杂的金融环境、监管要求以及对客户结果的直接影响相结合，造就了一种严谨胜于速度的实施环境。以下是你可能会遇到的一些挑战。

### Integrating with legacy infrastructure与遗留基础设施集成

Financial institutions typically run on decades-old core banking systems that weren't designed for real-time AI integration. Your loan origination system, trading platforms, and compliance databases often use different protocols, data formats, and security models.金融机构通常依赖数十年前开发的核心银行系统，这类系统并非为实时人工智能集成而设计。你的贷款发放系统、交易平台和合规数据库往往采用不同的协议、数据格式与安全模型。

Legacy system integration often shows up in:传统系统集成通常体现在以下方面：

- Core banking platform incompatibilities across vendors 不同供应商的核心银行平台不兼容
- Departmental data silos requiring cross-system orchestration 各部门的数据孤岛需要跨系统编排
- Legacy mainframe integration challenges 传统大型机集成挑战
- Real-time synchronization needs for time-sensitive trading decisions 对时效性强的交易决策的实时同步需求

When tackling these challenges, teams need to make practical decisions about integration approaches. The first consideration involves connectivity: does the AI agent have direct integration capabilities with the necessary systems? If not, teams face two practical options: building custom connectors (typically through APIs or MCP approaches) or implementing middleware systems to bridge these communication gaps.在应对这些挑战时，团队需要就集成方式做出切实可行的决策。首要考量点是连通性：该AI智能体是否具备与必要系统的直接集成能力？若不具备，团队将面临两种切实可行的选择：一是构建自定义连接器（通常通过API或MCP方式），二是部署中间件系统来弥合这些通信鸿沟。

For early agentic solutions, look to integrate with modern platforms with APIs and standard protocols. If you decide you need to connect to legacy systems, you'll need to develop middleware that can translate between these systems while maintaining transaction integrity and audit trails.对于早期的智能体解决方案，应考虑集成具备API和标准协议的现代平台。若你决定需要连接遗留系统，则需开发中间件，使其能在这些系统之间进行转换，同时保持事务完整性和审计追踪。

### Navigating regulatory complexity 应对监管复杂性

A single transaction might trigger compliance requirements from multiple regulators, including SEC, FDIC, state banking authorities, and international bodies for cross-border payments. AI agents must understand not just what actions to take, but which regulatory frameworks apply to each decision and how to document actions for different audit requirements.一笔交易可能会触发多个监管机构的合规要求，包括美国证券交易委员会（SEC）、美国联邦存款保险公司（FDIC）、州银行监管机构以及处理跨境支付的国际机构。智能体不仅必须清楚应采取哪些行动，还需了解每项决策适用哪些监管框架，以及如何针对不同的审计要求记录相关操作。

Some regulatory considerations that need to be part of your agent architecture include:你的智能体架构中需要纳入的一些监管考量包括：

- SOC 2 and PCI DSS compliance for AI data processing workflows AI数据处理工作流的SOC 2和PCI DSS合规性
- Evidence-based validation of risk assessment accuracy 基于证据验证风险评估的准确性
- Documentation requirements for AI decision audit trails AI 决策审计跟踪的文档要求

Ensure you build observability and traceability into the agentic solutions from day one. You'll want it simply from a troubleshooting perspective, but you'll definitely need it from a regulatory one.从一开始就要在智能体解决方案中构建可观测性和可追溯性。仅从故障排查的角度，你就需要具备这两项能力；而从合规的角度来看，你更是必须拥有它们。

### Managing real-time risk 管理实时风险

Unlike other industries where decisions can be reviewed later, financial agents often make choices that immediately impact customer accounts, market positions, or regulatory standing. This demands fail-safe architectures where agents can act quickly but always within predefined risk parameters that protect both customers and the institution.与其他行业可在后续复核决策不同，金融智能体做出的选择往往会立即影响客户账户、市场头寸或合规状态。这就要求构建故障安全架构，让智能体能够快速行动，但始终在预设的风险参数范围内行事，从而同时保障客户和机构的利益。

Implementation requires: 实施要求：

- Transparent reasoning that financial professionals can validate and explain to clients 金融专业人员可验证并能向客户解释的透明推理逻辑
- Clear escalation pathways for complex or ambiguous financial situations 针对复杂或模糊的财务情况制定明确的升级路径
- Override capabilities allowing advisors to reject AI recommendations when client circumstances warrant 覆盖功能，允许顾问在客户情况需要时拒绝人工智能的建议
- Fail-safe defaults that prioritize customer protection over operational efficiency 优先保障客户权益而非运营效率的故障安全默认设置

Identify which actions will require human-in-the-loop authorization, either from a risk or regulatory perspective. For high-risk actions, consider how the system, agentic or otherwise, can fail in a known safe state.从风险或监管角度，确定哪些操作需要人工介入授权。对于高风险操作，需考虑系统（无论是否具备智能体特性）如何在已知的安全状态下发生故障。

## Practical deployment strategies 实用部署策略

AI agents are already transforming financial operations for some companies. There are abundant examples of agents currently in production and delivering measurable impact on fraud detection, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency. So, how do you build and deploy agents that address your specific operational challenges, while making sure they meet regulatory requirements and risk management standards?AI智能体已在为部分企业重塑金融运营模式。目前已有大量投入实际应用的智能体案例，它们在欺诈检测、客户满意度提升和运营效率优化方面取得了可量化的成效。那么，企业应如何构建和部署智能体，以解决自身特定的运营难题，同时确保这些智能体符合监管要求与风险管理标准呢？

### Finding your starting point 找到你的切入点

The best agent initiatives begin by targeting the things that everyone already agrees need fixing. Clear metrics make all the difference here, because they show whether the solution actually works and help build momentum for wider adoption.最优秀的智能体项目都始于瞄准那些所有人都公认需要解决的问题。清晰的指标在此至关重要，因为它们能验证解决方案是否切实有效，并助力为更广泛的推广积累势头。

#### Choose high-impact, low-risk targets选择高影响力、低风险的目标

Target opportunities for adding agents within your organization to areas where the stakes are manageable, but the potential impact is meaningful.在组织内为智能体增设部署目标时，应优先选择风险可控但潜在影响显著的领域。

Look for processes where human oversight already exists or where consequences of imperfect automation remain minimal: these are perfect for early adoption without introducing excessive organizational risk. Customer service triage, internal knowledge retrieval, and routine data validation are natural entry points where AI agents can immediately reduce workloads while humans remain in the verification loop.寻找那些已有人工监督、或不完善的自动化所带来的后果仍处于最低水平的流程：这些流程非常适合在早期采用，且不会带来过多的组织风险。客户服务分诊、内部知识检索以及常规数据验证都是自然的切入点，在这些环节中，AI 智能体可以立即减轻工作负担，同时让人类仍处于验证环节中。

Beyond immediate productivity gains, these initial agent deployments are extremely valuable learning experiences. Each low-risk implementation helps your teams develop practical understanding of agent capabilities, limitation patterns, and integration requirements without the pressure of mission-critical deadlines. Your technical staff learns to fine-tune prompts and monitoring systems in environments where mistakes are learning opportunities rather than costly errors.除了直接提升生产力之外，这些初期的智能体部署也是极具价值的学习实践。每一次低风险的落地实施，都能让团队在无需承担关键任务截止日期压力的情况下，切实掌握智能体的能力、局限性模式以及集成需求。技术人员能够在失误可作为学习契机而非代价高昂的错误的环境中，学会微调提示词与监控系统。

#### Start simple, learn fast 从简单开始，快速学习

Start with agents that handle one straightforward task, like flagging unusual transaction patterns, monitoring compliance deadlines, or automating document classification.从那些只处理一项简单任务的智能体入手，比如标记异常交易模式、监控合规截止日期或自动完成文档分类。

You'll get tangible operational improvements while keeping human judgment firmly in the loop while building the organizational muscle memory needed for more ambitious agent applications. When you eventually tackle higher-risk use cases, you'll have technical capabilities and confidence built through experience rather than theoretical assumptions about how agents might perform in your specific environment.你将获得切实的运营效率提升，同时牢牢保留人工判断的核心地位，并培养出构建更具雄心的智能体应用所需的组织能力惯性。当你最终着手处理更高风险的应用场景时，所具备的技术能力和信心都将源于实践经验，而非基于智能体在你特定环境中可能表现的理论假设。

### Scaling across the organization 在全组织范围内推广应用

Success with initial implementations opens the door to enterprise-wide capabilities. The key is moving from point solutions to shared infrastructure that serves multiple departments.初步实施取得成功将为企业级功能的落地打开大门。关键在于从单点解决方案转向可服务多个部门的共享基础设施。

#### Building reusable foundations 构建可复用的基础

Your organization will get better results when you build foundational AI agent capabilities that serve multiple departments rather than deploying one-off solutions for individual problems.当你的组织构建能服务多个部门的基础人工智能智能体能力，而非针对单个问题部署一次性解决方案时，会取得更好的效果。

For example, you might implement a document processing capability that could be used across your organization. The same AI system that automates [bank reconciliations](https://www.claude.com/customers/campfire) and invoice processing can help compliance teams analyze regulatory documents and support various departments with financial data extraction. Each department contributes use cases that strengthen the core capability while benefiting from improvements driven by other teams.例如，你可以实现一项可在整个组织内使用的文档处理功能。实现 [银行对账](https://www.claude.com/customers/campfire) 和发票处理自动化的同一套人工智能系统，还能帮助合规团队分析监管文件，并为各部门提供财务数据提取支持。每个部门都能提供用例来强化核心功能，同时也能受益于其他团队推动的功能改进。

#### Earning trust with stakeholders 获取利益相关方的信任

Agents interact with both your workforce and your customers, and each group will respond differently to AI-assisted processes. Trust-building matters as much as technical deployment.智能体与你的员工和客户都要进行交互，而这两类群体对人工智能辅助流程的反应各不相同。建立信任与技术部署同样重要。

For customers, transparency matters. Make it clear when they're interacting with an AI agent versus a human, explain what the agent can and cannot do, and provide straightforward pathways to human specialists when needed. This clarity builds confidence and encourages broader adoption of AI-assisted services.对客户而言，透明度至关重要。要明确告知客户他们是在与智能体还是人类互动，说明智能体的能力范围，并在客户需要时提供直接联系人类专业人员的途径。这种清晰的指引能建立客户信任，推动AI辅助服务的更广泛应用。

Internal adoption follows similar principles. Your organization already has change management processes for new systems. Apply them here.
